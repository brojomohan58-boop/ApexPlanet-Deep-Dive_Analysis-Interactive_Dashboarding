The objective throughout the internship is to transform raw sales data into meaningful and actionable business insights using industry-standard analytics tools and techniques.

# 🎯 Task 3 — Core KPIs

The interactive Power BI dashboard focuses on core business performance indicators.

### 1. Total Revenue

**Formula:**

```text
Total Revenue = SUM(Total_Sales)
```

**Business Rationale:**

Measures the overall revenue generated by the business and provides a high-level view of sales performance.

---

### 2. Total Orders

**Formula:**

```text
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Order_ID)
```

**Business Rationale:**

Measures the number of unique transactions and helps evaluate overall sales activity.

---

### 3. Total Customers

**Formula:**

```text
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(Customer_ID)
```

**Business Rationale:**

Measures the size of the customer base and provides important context for revenue and order performance.

---

### 4. Average Order Value

**Formula:**

```text
AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders
```

**Business Rationale:**

Measures the average revenue generated per order and helps understand customer purchasing value.

# 🔬 Task 3 — Deep-Dive Analysis

## Customer Segmentation — RFM Analysis

The deep-dive analysis uses **RFM methodology** to understand customer behavior and value.

### RFM Dimensions

| Dimension | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Recency** | How recently a customer made a purchase |
| **Frequency** | How frequently a customer made purchases |
| **Monetary** | How much revenue a customer generated |

These dimensions are combined to classify customers into meaningful business segments.

---

# 👥 Customer Segments

The Power BI dashboard analyzes customer groups including:

- Champions

- Other RFM-based customer segments

The segmentation enables analysis of customer value, engagement, and potential retention opportunities.
